Our story

30 years of sport,
friendship and beer

Prague's Barrel started in 1993 as a small friendly football tournament. Today we organise around ten tournaments a year in three sports, and over three decades we have welcomed more than 20,000 participants from 40 countries — from Iceland to Israel, from Canada to Chile.

The idea has never changed: amateur teams, fair play, perfect organisation and a city that turns every tournament into a holiday. Most of our teams come back — and that is the only statistic we really care about.
